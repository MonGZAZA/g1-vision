5–12 s of one person speaking clearly, plus exactly what is said. Only with that person's consent. The clone copies the voice and the tone: read in the friendly, polite tone the robot should use.
reading script
how to record
  • quiet room, no music, fan or echo (curtains or a sofa help); one speaker only
  • microphone (phone or headset) 20–30 cm from the mouth, a little to the side; do not touch it
  • wait half a second, read calmly in one take, wait half a second, stop
  • if a word is read differently, correct the transcript below so it matches exactly; numbers as words
  • record 2–3 takes or sentences under different names, compare them with Say, keep the best
